# Review — BrAIan detail film

Five real product views tell a short tour: own the files, see plans, read feeds, follow connections, ask the vault. Bold opaque navy cards separate each claim from the actual UI. No generated app screens or decorative device motion. The separate slideshow handles browsing; this film handles detail and pace.

Reviewed the ten beat frames, phone-width sheet, and cut strips. Camera changes are substantial (up to 1.72× with 300px pan), the five short claims hold for about 2.5 seconds, and the final command remains visible through the final shot. Small labels, brand and command were raised to 28–30px after the first check. Layout, runtime and contrast checks passed; the final check follows that type change.

Cuts deliberately reset the camera between features. First frame shows real files; text enters after 0.08 seconds. No blank seam was found in the reviewed strips. Phone-width claims remain readable; the UI is contextual rather than tutorial-sized.

Weakest shot: Chat contains more whitespace than the other screens. It is a real short conversation; the close-up starts on its answer and pulls back to the input rather than inventing more content. A longer product demo could capture an extended conversation.

Silent by design: 14.5 seconds, muted inline webpage feature tour. No sound was mixed or heard. No warnings intentionally retained. Delivery uses a web-compressed MP4; editable scenes and original captures are retained.
